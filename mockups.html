Settings — Design Proposals
Current problem: long vertical list of sections with large spacing = lots of scrolling. Two options below.
Option A
Compact Grouped List
Standard iOS/Android settings pattern. Profile card stays prominent, then settings in tight grouped rows with icons. Dramatically less space per row.
  • Familiar — users know how to read it instantly
  • 8-10 settings visible without scrolling
  • Clear visual grouping by category label
  • Switches visible inline (no tap needed)
  • Member list compact — avatars not 44px anymore
  • Less dramatic / premium feel than current design
