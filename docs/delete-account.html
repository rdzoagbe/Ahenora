Delete your Household COO account

Household COO, by Dzoagbe Labs · Last updated: July 2026

You can ask us to delete your Household COO account and its associated data at any time. There are two ways to do it — both reach the same place.

Option 1 — From inside the app

  1. Open Household COO.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete account.
  4. Enter the email address used for your account, add an optional reason, and submit the request.

Option 2 — By email

Send a deletion request to rolanddzoagbe@gmail.com from (or quoting) the email address used for your Household COO account, so the request can be matched to the correct account.

What gets deleted

When your deletion request is processed, the following data associated with your account is permanently deleted:

DataOutcome
Account profile — name, email address, account identifiers, password hashDeleted
Household content — tasks, cards, notes, announcements, shopping lists, meal plansDeleted
Children profiles, stars, rewards and PIN settingsDeleted
Vault documents and scanned imagesDeleted
Imported calendar events stored in the appDeleted
Notification settings and device push tokensDeleted
Sign-in sessionsRevoked and deleted

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Shared household content. If you share a household with another parent or member, content they created — or shared items the household still depends on — remains with that household. Your personal account and the content you own are still deleted.
  • Purchase and subscription records. Transactions are handled by Google Play. Billing records may be retained by Google and by us where required for tax, accounting and fraud-prevention obligations, typically for up to 10 years as required by law.
  • Backups. Deleted data may persist in encrypted database backups for up to 30 days before those backups expire and are overwritten.
  • Support correspondence. Emails you send us may be retained for up to 12 months for support and dispute-handling purposes.

How long it takes

Deletion requests are actioned within 30 days of receipt. You will receive a confirmation at the email address associated with your account once the deletion has been completed.

Cancelling your subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a paid subscription. If you have Household COO Premium, cancel it in the Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions before or after deleting your account.

Questions

For any question about deletion or your data, contact rolanddzoagbe@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
