# TikTok flyers — text, captions and hashtags

Five flyers, five different reasons someone downloads this app. Each one is
tied to a feature that actually ships; nothing here promises something the
app does not do.

The rule these follow: **TikTok rewards specificity over polish.** "Organise
your family life" scrolls past. "I thought YOU were picking her up" stops a
thumb, because a real person has said that sentence out loud in their own
kitchen. Every hook below is a moment, not a benefit.

The rendered PNGs live in `docs/store-assets/tiktok/`, 1080x1920, ready to
post. They are generated by `scripts/make_flyers.py` — edit the copy there and
re-run it rather than retouching an exported image, so the file and this
document never drift apart.

## How they are built

- **9:16 vertical**, content held in the upper two thirds so the caption,
  username and buttons TikTok overlays at the bottom never cover the words.
- **Two lines with a break between them.** Line one is the situation, line two
  is the turn. That gap is the whole design — it is where the viewer's brain
  finishes the joke before the flyer does.
- **Cream `#F6F3EE`, charcoal `#26221E`, deep orange `#C2410C`.** The same ink
  the app uses, so the flyer and the first screen after install feel like the
  same object.
- **No stock photos, no screenshots.** A screenshot asks the viewer to squint
  at a UI they have no reason to care about yet.
- **A small orange kicker above the hook.** It names the theme in three words
  so the flyer is legible even to somebody who scrolls past before reading
  the punchline — and it never repeats a word from the lines below it.

## Posting notes

- One flyer per post. Bundling them dilutes the hook.
- Post the **privacy** one first — it is the sharpest and the most argued-with
  in the comments, and arguments are reach.
- Read the two lines aloud over the still, with a 1.5s pause on the break. A
  static image with a voice beats a static image.
- Reply to comments with the specific feature, not a sales line. "It's per
  item — you tick 'share' or you don't" outperforms "check out our privacy
  features!"

## A note on hashtag mix

Each set deliberately blends three tiers:

1. **Broad reach** (`#momlife`, `#relatable`) — huge, competitive, cheap to enter.
2. **Topic** (`#chorechart`, `#sharedcalendar`) — where people actually search.
3. **Feeling** (`#mentalload`, `#defaultparent`) — small, loyal, high-intent.

The third tier is where installs come from. Do not drop it in favour of more
of the first.
