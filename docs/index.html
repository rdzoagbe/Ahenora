The household runs on someone's memory. Let it run on something calmer.

Ahenora holds tasks, the family calendar, kids' chores, meals and school paperwork in one shared, private place — so family life never depends on one person remembering all of it. For solo parents, co-parents across two homes, and every busy household in between.

What's inside

Everything a household actually needs

Not another to-do list — the specific things that quietly fall through the cracks in a busy family.

📋

Tasks that come back

Sign slips, RSVPs, bin night. Assign them, set reminders, and let recurring routines return on their own.

📅

One shared calendar

Import Google or Outlook so events become actionable cards. Everyone sees the same week.

⭐

Kids, chores and stars

Chores with stars, rewards and pocket money — so routines stick without the daily negotiation.

🍽️

Meals, shopping and what it cost

Plan the week's dinners — or ask the AI for a recipe — and turn it all into one shared shopping list. Log what each shop cost and see where the money actually goes, by month and by shop.

📄

Scan the paperwork

Photograph a school letter or appointment card and turn it straight into a task or a saved document.

🔒

A vault for documents

Passports, insurance, medical records — organised by category, private to your household.

💬

Family messages NEW

Talk to your co-parent, your teen or the childminder without leaving the app — and write notes your younger children read on the family phone. Every private conversation includes a parent, by design.

🧑‍🎓

Teen accounts NEW

Give your 13–17-year-old their own private space — just their tasks and shared events, nothing else of the family's. They earn stars you approve.
