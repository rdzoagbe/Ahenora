# The 3D faces

People's pictures in Ahenora (`src/faces.ts`), one face per file, 384 × 384 WebP.

## Where they come from

Generated by Roland Dzoagbe with **Google Gemini**, on his own Google account,
from prompts written for Ahenora (four sheets of six faces, 2026-11-03).
Roland confirmed this in writing on 2026-11-04: "yes I made them in Gemini on
my account". Each sheet was cut into separate faces with Python (Pillow); no
other change was made.

Google's Terms of Service (effective 30 July 2026), "Your content": "Some of
our services allow you to generate original content. Google won't claim
ownership over that content." The prompts and the faces are not anyone else's
work: no reference photo, artwork or real person was given to the generator.

Images made from a text prompt alone have no copyright owner in most places
(US Copyright Office, Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2, January
2025), so nobody can stop Ahenora using them, and Ahenora cannot stop anyone
copying them. For profile pictures that is fine.

## Adding more

Generate the new sheet the same way, in the same style, and add one entry per
face to `FACES` in `src/faces.ts`. Never rename or remove an id: a member's
stored picture (`face:<id>`) must always point at a file.
